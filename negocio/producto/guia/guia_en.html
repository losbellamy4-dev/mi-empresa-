How to use your templates in 5 steps

  1. 1
    Open a link
    Click one of the templates in the table. Canva opens in your browser. If you don't have an account, create a free one: that's all you need.
  2. 2
    Click “Use template”
    Canva makes a copy in your account. Your changes are yours; the original stays untouched and you can reuse it as often as you like.
  3. 3
    Swap the photo
    Drag your listing photo onto the photo frame. It crops automatically. Double-click to move or resize it.
  4. 4
    Type your details
    Click any text in brackets, such as [Neighborhood] or [Price], and replace it. Put your logo in the LOGO box.
  5. 5
    Download and post
    Share → Download → PNG. Post it on Instagram. For the carousel, download all pages and upload them together.

Tips

  • Your colors: click a color and pick your brand color. “Change all” updates it across the whole design.
  • 3 palettes: every template comes in beige, navy and sage. Pick one and stick to it so your feed looks consistent.
  • Calendar: open the “30-Day Posting Calendar” PDF. It tells you which template to post each day. Start with day 1.
  • Photos: use your own photos or free stock photos with a commercial license. Photos shown in the previews are not included.
